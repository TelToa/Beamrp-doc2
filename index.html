Section 2
Getting Started

Getting Whitelisted

In order to get whitelisted into the BeamRP server, you must complete the Whitelist application. After you submit the form, a staff member will look at your application and notify you if you passed or failed. It may take a while for them to get to your application, please be patient.

Joining the server

In the BeamMP server menu, search for “BeamRP” and join one of the Roleplay or Freeroam servers.

If the server does not appear:

  1. Navigate to the “Direct Connect” tab in the multiplayer menu.

Bot Commands

BeamRP uses a Discord bot for economy commands. Use them in #bot-commands.

/initialize

Initializes you into the system. Use it to create your first character or start a new one upon death. Starts with $5,000.

Arguments: user (Your BeamMP username)
/initialize
/status

Shows your current balance, license status, and blacklist status.

Arguments: [User] (Optional)
/status
/status @itsaname
/addmoney

Adds money to your character balance.

Arguments: Amount, Reason
/addmoney 200 Two deliveries
/addmoney 700 Got paid by @gitty
/delmoney

Removes money from your balance when making purchases or paying fees.

Arguments: Amount, Reason
/delmoney 6000 Bought ETK K-Series KC4 (M)
/delmoney 15000 Paying fines
/delmoney 700 Paid @itsaname

Section 3
Vehicles

Your first vehicle

You're ready to buy your first vehicle! You only have $5,000, so you can't buy anything fancy yet. You can filter the price of vehicles in the vehicle spawn menu by going to the filters section on the right, opening the "value" section, and setting the maximum price you are looking for.


Note: Check "Expand Configurations" to see all vehicles that fit your range.

Each vehicle has a tier. Consider the tier of the vehicle you are buying, as it affects the price of insurance and the cost of parts.

Once you have decided which vehicle you want to buy, go to #bot-commands and use the /delmoney command to remove the money from your balance. Make sure to include the vehicle you're buying in the reason section.

Insuring your vehicle

Insurance will cover part of your bill whenever you get into an accident or crash, however you will lose your current insurance if you get into a crash. Insurance cost depends on the tier of your vehicle based on the following rates:

Tier Price
Low tier $500
Mid tier $850
High tier $1000
+High tier $1250
Classics $650

Insurance applies for the day that you are playing, and will have to be re-bought the next day.

Modifying your vehicle

In order to modify your vehicle, check the price of the part(s) you want to buy in the parts price spreadsheet. You don't have to go to a repair shop for repair, however for roleplay purposes it is recommended to go to either a shop or garage.

There are no prices for modded cars currently, so use your best judgement.

Repairing your vehicle

Repairing your vehicle requires you to be able to drive to a repair shop and purchase the part(s) that were broken.

Neither major nor frame damage can be repaired. If your vehicle has major damage, you must "restore" it.

Restoring your vehicle

You can restore damaged vehicles in two ways:

  1. By yourself: You are only able to restore a vehicle yourself if you can transport the new parts to your vehicle. Drive your vehicle to the auto repair shop, or a repair shop you have permission to restore in. Buy all the parts you need, and transport them from the harbor to your vehicle.
  2. Paying a company: Restoration companies get a 50% discount on parts. The total cost will include labor fees for the restoration.
