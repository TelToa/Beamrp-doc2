Taxi Driver
Unlocked at delivery level 2

Section 1
Roleplay

The taxi driver's role is to drive around the county picking up either real or pretend passengers

  • Find a house/business: Open the door for passengers
  • Drive cautiously: Avoid upsetting passengers
