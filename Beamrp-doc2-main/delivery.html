Delivery Driver

Section 1
Roleplay

It is up to each player to decide how to realistically roleplay their job. However, standard community expectations for delivery driver roleplay include:

  • Stop at Delivery Locations: Pause for a few seconds at designated drop-off points.
  • Drive Safely: Maintain vehicle control to prevent damaging goods or risking traffic fines.
  • Physical Delivery (Optional): Stepping out of your vehicle to unload goods enhances roleplay immersion.
