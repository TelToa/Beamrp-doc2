Bus Driver
Unlocked at delivery level 2

Section 1
Roleplay

Drive between bus stops to keep the people of Firwood County going.

  • Deport departure: Begin at the bus depot (The gated section in the alleyway).
  • Follow the route: Follow the bus routes provided
  • Stop at Delivery Locations: Pause for a few seconds at designated drop-off points.

    • Section 2
    Server Limits

    To ensure server stability and balance, bus drivers must adhere to the following slot limits and cooldowns:

    Active Bus Slot Limit

    Maximum Permitted: 3 Buses

    If 3 buses are already active, someone must quit the job before a new slot becomes available.

    Shift Limit & Cooldown

    Max Stops per Shift: 30 Stops

    Mandatory Cooldown: 30 IRL Minutes after reaching 30 stops.
